Chicken Karahi

About This Dish

Chicken Karahi is a quick, spicy, tomato-based dish cooked in a wok-style pan known as "karahi". It is famous for its bold flavours and aroma.

Includes:

  • Fresh tomatoes
  • Green chillies
  • Ginger & garlic
  • Traditional spices
