Chicken Biryani

About this dish

Chicken Biryani is a rich and aromatic rise dish made with layered basmati rice, marinated chicken, and traditional spices. It is one of the most iconic dishes in South Asia

Main Ingredients:

  • Basmati rice
  • Chicken Pieces
  • Yougurt & spices
  • Friend onions
  • Fresh coriandar & mint
